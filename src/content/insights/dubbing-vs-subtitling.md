The right localization format depends on how, where, and why an audience will watch. Dubbing can create a more immediate experience, while subtitles preserve the original performance and can be faster to produce.

## Start with audience context

Consider language fluency, screen size, accessibility, genre, distribution platform, and whether the audience is likely to multitask while watching.
