01 The studio

REVERB Inc. is a Bengaluru-based language and content studio, founded by Badekkila Pradeep — a multilingual voice artist, producer, and media professional with two decades across voice, screen, and storytelling.

Pradeep is the official voice of multiple Kannada television channels, and the English voice you hear on Namma Metro, Bengaluru.

We are not a translation agency. Not just a dubbing house. We are a content and language partner — one that understands media deeply, moves fast, and delivers work that actually connects with the audience it’s made for. Our talent network of native-language artists and vendors spans India and beyond.
